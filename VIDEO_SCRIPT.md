# CHIP — 3:00 demo video script

Teleprompter format. Nine pods of twenty seconds. Read at a natural pace; ~150 words per minute lands you on time. Cut hard at 2:59.

Tone: spare, declarative, JARVIS-adjacent. Don't oversell. The work has to do the work.

---

## Act 1 — The problem (0:00–1:00)

### Pod 1 — 0:00–0:20 · Personal hook

> "I'm a design systems engineer. Self-employed. Single mom. I run BELLA — my own design system — across Web, iOS, and Android. Last sprint the platforms drifted: Web shipped landscape mode, iOS half-shipped, Android didn't start. Parity dropped to thirty-eight percent. I built a tool to fix it."

**Screen:** Splash screen. *"Morning, Elleta."* Three things on your plate. Hold on the splash for the first ten seconds. At "I built a tool," click *Enter Bridge* — the Bridge lands.

---

### Pod 2 — 0:20–0:40 · The pain

> "This is the design systems lead's nightmare. Twenty-three components out of parity. Three squads waiting on me to coordinate. Thirty-day audit cycles. By the time I find the drift, it's already a quarter behind. The tool I need doesn't answer questions. It holds the decision-making moment in view."

**Screen:** Bridge space visible. Headline reads *BELLA parity is 38% across Web, iOS, Android*. Mouse hovers over the parity meter. Pan slowly down past the four stat cards (Pending · Inbox · Today · Coming up).

---

### Pod 3 — 0:40–1:00 · Why existing tools fail

> "Existing tools are retrospective. They score past drift. They generate specs after the fact. Vitaly Friedman's principle: expert-user dashboards should surface judgment at the moment of decision, not a scorecard after the fact. CHIP is that dashboard. We intervene at the moment decisions are made."

**Screen:** Stop scrolling. Hold on the parity meter and the action line: *"Action — review the roadmap below, press Approve to file 23 Jira tickets and notify Web/iOS/Android leads."* Let the headline sit.

---

## Act 2 — The Bridge (1:00–2:00)

### Pod 4 — 1:00–1:20 · The landing

> "Here's the Bridge. Bento layout. Parity at thirty-eight percent. Agent card watching every surface. Four stat cards. Priorities, calendar, pending replies. Surfaces shelf with sparklines. System map and research library in the sidebar. Every pixel of this was built by Opus 4.7. In five days. From specs I wrote in plain English."

**Screen:** Wide shot of the bento. Mouse traces the hero row (meter + agent), drops to the four stat cards, then briefly past the surfaces shelf with the live sparklines. Don't click anything yet. Let the layout breathe.

---

### Pod 5 — 1:20–1:40 · Open audit

> "I click Open audit. The proposal lands. Twenty-three components to align. Confidence ninety-five. Inclusion check: pass. Trust level: junior — requires explicit approve. Below it: Vitaly's readiness pattern. What's done. What's to do. What's ready. Estimated time: nine minutes. I see my preparation state before I commit."

**Screen:** Click *Open audit*. The diag banner expands. Persistent strip pins under the topbar: *Parity 38% · Web ✓ · iOS ◐ · Android ◐ · Monitor → Analyze*. Scroll down to the readiness card. Pause on the three columns.

---

### Pod 6 — 1:40–2:00 · Approve

> "I press A. The to-do items migrate to done. Twenty-three Jira tickets file themselves. Three dev leads notified. Meter tweens to ninety-four. The audit ribbon records it. Roadmap approved. Trust level junior. Auto-merge withheld. The decision is logged. The system held the moment."

**Screen:** Press <kbd>A</kbd>. Watch the readiness To-do items flip to Done. The meter animates 38 → 94. The persistent strip phase shifts Monitor → Analyze → Execute → Knowledge. Scroll to the audit ribbon. Show the new entry.

---

## Act 3 — The system underneath (2:00–3:00)

### Pod 7 — 2:00–2:20 · System Map + Simulation

> "This is the system underneath. Atomic concentric rings — Tokens at the core, Atoms, Organisms. Each component plotted. Click Button. Five tabs open: Overview. Implications — what changes if I change this. Changes. Simulation — what-if sliders, live cost and compliance. AI Insights. Per-component health, not system averages."

**Screen:** Click *System map* in the sidebar. Atomic rings render. Click the Button node on the Atoms ring. Detail panel populates. Click the *Simulation* tab. Drag the hours slider. The cost / compliance / workload / tickets cards update live. Click *AI Insights*. Show the five severity-tagged cards and the letter grade.

---

### Pod 8 — 2:20–2:40 · Palette + lineage

> "Cmd+K from anywhere. Fifty surfaces indexed in one keystroke. And at the bottom: the lineage. Maggie Appleton's daemon. Projects By IF's trust patterns. Shape of AI's vocabulary. Wattenberger's embeddings. Wroblewski's structured search. Vitaly's expert-user dashboard. Every named pattern is in the prototype."

**Screen:** Press <kbd>⌘K</kbd>. Palette opens at 12vh. Type `appleton` — Maggie Appleton entry surfaces. Press Esc. Switch back to Bridge. Scroll all the way down to the lineage citation, just above the audit ribbon.

---

### Pod 9 — 2:40–3:00 · The meta beat

> "Built by Opus 4.7. Two Claude instances — terminal and Cowork — writing to a single HANDOFF log in alternating blocks. One instance builds. The other reviews and writes the next task. Eighteen stages. Seven thousand lines. The build process is the thesis. CHIP holds the decision moment. So did Claude."

**Screen:** Open Settings → *About CHIP*. Modal renders the museum-placard view: tagline, brain Opus 4.7, the full lineage paragraph. Hold on the modal until 2:59. Cut.

---

## Recording notes

- Practice once at full speed before recording. Mouse work is choreographed.
- If a pod runs long, drop the second-shortest sentence — never the headline.
- Don't read the kbd brackets aloud (`<kbd>A</kbd>` → "A").
- Speak the percentages as words ("thirty-eight percent" not "38%").
- The persistent strip pinning under the topbar is the visual proof of "the decision-making moment is held in view." Make sure the camera sees it.
- "Bridge" is the space name in the sidebar nav. The splash button reads "Enter Bridge."
